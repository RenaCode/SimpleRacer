3D models in this folder come from three asset packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl):

- Cars/  — Car Kit 3.1           https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit
- City/  — City Kit (Commercial) 2.1  https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial
- Track/ — Racing Kit 1.0        https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
They can be used for personal, educational and commercial purposes. Attribution is not
required; credit to Kenney is given here because it is appreciated.

To swap a model, replace the .obj/.mtl (and texture) with another file of the same name.
Cars are matched in RaceCore/Sources/RaceCore/CarCatalog.swift (`CarModelRef`).
